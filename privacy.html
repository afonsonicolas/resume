Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

This site, nafonsocv.com, is the personal portfolio of Nicolas Afonso. I take a deliberately minimal, privacy-first approach to measuring how the site is used. This page explains exactly what is and isn't collected, why, and the choices you have.

The short version: No advertising. No third-party trackers. No cookies. No personal data (no names, emails, phone numbers, or precise location). Analytics only run after you opt in, and you can change your mind at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Nicolas Afonso is the data controller for this site. For any privacy question or request, contact nico.afonso1207@gmail.com.

2. What is collected

If — and only if — you accept analytics, the site records anonymous, aggregate usage events to understand which projects and pages are viewed. Each event may include:

  • Page activity: the page path and title, pages viewed, and time engaged on the page.
  • Interactions: which projects, certifications, references, resume, and contact links are opened, scroll depth, and outbound link destinations (host only).
  • Technical context: approximate device type (mobile/tablet/desktop), viewport size, device pixel ratio, browser language, and time zone.
  • Source: the host name of the site that referred you (for example, linkedin.com) and any campaign tags (UTM parameters) present in the link you followed. Full referrer URLs are never stored.
  • A random session ID that exists only for the current browsing session (see “Storage” below).

What is not collected

  • No names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No precise/geolocation data and no storage of your IP address by this site.
  • No cookies and no persistent cross-visit or cross-site identifiers.
  • No fingerprinting, no advertising profiles, and no data brokers.

3. How the data is handled

The analytics are first-party and contain no third-party code. At present, usage events are stored only in your own browser (in localStorage) and are not transmitted to any server. If first-party server collection is enabled in the future, the same anonymous events described above would be sent to an endpoint operated solely for this site — never to advertising networks or third parties — and this policy would be updated accordingly. As is standard for any web request, a hosting/CDN provider may process your IP address transiently to deliver the page and protect against abuse; this site does not retain it.

5. Legal basis & your choices

Where the GDPR / UK GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive apply, the legal basis for analytics is your consent, requested via the banner before anything is recorded. You can:

  • Decline — no analytics run and nothing is stored beyond your choice.
  • Withdraw consent at any time via the “Privacy choices” link in the footer or the button above; doing so stops analytics and deletes the local event buffer.
  • Rely on your browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal — if either is on, analytics are disabled automatically and you won't see the banner.

Because no personal data is collected, there is no sale or sharing of personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). Residents may still contact me with any request.

6. Data retention

The session identifier is discarded when your session ends. The local event buffer is capped and overwrites its oldest entries; it is deleted entirely if you withdraw consent or clear your browser storage.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since this site does not hold personally identifying information, most requests can be fully satisfied by declining/withdrawing consent and clearing your browser storage. For anything else, email nico.afonso1207@gmail.com.

8. External links

This site links to external resources (e.g., LinkedIn, YouTube, project demos). Those services have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy materially changes, the “Last updated” date will change and, where required, consent will be requested again.
